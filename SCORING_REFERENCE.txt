  1.  SCORING FACTORS (additive, 0–100 scale)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  FACTOR                  WEIGHT   CONDITION
  ──────────────────────  ──────   ──────────────────────────────
  MTF Aligned              +25    3+ timeframes agree (4H/1D/1W)
  Recent Compression       +20    comp_score >= 2 within last 3 bars
  Fresh 4H Flip            +20    flip_age <= 2 bars (8 hours)
  ALMA Momentum            +10    ALMA direction confirms signal
  RVOL (Volume Surge)      +10    vol_ratio >= 1.2x 20-bar avg
  Sector Strength          +10    Sector ETF trend aligned
  Relative Strength        +10    Stock outperforming SPY (5-day)
                          ─────
  MAX POSSIBLE             105    (clamped to 100)

  2.  PENALTIES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  PENALTY                 VALUE    CONDITION
  ──────────────────────  ──────   ──────────────────────────────
  Mid Extension            -20    extATR > 2.5
  High Extension           -40    extATR > 3.5 (replaces -20)
  Earnings Proximity       -25    Earnings within 7 days
  Weak Market Regime       -15    SPY below trail (bull signal)

  HARD SUPPRESS:  extATR > 4.0 → score forced to 0
